A friendly, safer place to write

Built with care from day one.

  • Site rooms are automatically moderated: no links, slurs blocked, and repeat trouble earns an automatic mute.
  • Block or ignore anyone, report anything, and choose who can see your profile, friends and gifts.
  • Adults only: every member is 18 or older, checked at signup. Real ages and birthdates are never shown.
  • Characters can be any age, but sexual content involving characters under 18 is never allowed.
  • Two-factor sign-in and confirmed emails keep accounts yours.
