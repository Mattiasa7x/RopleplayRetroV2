1.Who we are

RoleplayRetro ("we", "us") runs roleplayretro.com (the "Service"), a free, adults-only roleplay and chat site. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

2.What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your member name, email address and password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash that nobody, including us, can read.
  • Birthday: your real birthdate, used only to confirm you're 18 or older. It is never shown and can't be changed.
  • Phone number (optional): if you add one for account security.
  • Two-factor sign-in: if you turn it on, your authenticator secret and backup codes. The backup codes are stored in hashed form.
  • Invite code: if someone invited you, we record who, so they get credit.
  • Gold Quill purchases: if you buy a pass, we keep the PayPal order number, which pass, the amount, the date and whether it completed. Your card or bank details go to PayPal only; we never see them.
  • Your content: room messages, private messages, profile and character sheet, statuses, comments, photos, banners, gifts and gift messages, rooms you create, and reports you file.

Information collected automatically

  • Security signals: your network address and a random device identifier. We turn these into one-way scrambled codes (HMAC hashes) before storing them. We use them to stop spam, limit fake accounts and enforce bans. We never store your raw IP address. When you sign up, and while an account is new or has been banned before, we also check your address against a public list of known VPN and proxy addresses kept on our own server; the address isn't stored or sent to anyone for this.
  • Sessions: when you're signed in and on which devices, so you can see and sign out your devices in Settings.
  • Last active: when you last used the site, to apply the 2-year inactivity rule.
  • Activity on the site: things like whether you're online right now, which rooms you favourited, how many messages you've sent (for trophies), and which profiles you've viewed (shown to that profile's owner).
  • Server logs: our host keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (such as the page requested and the time) to keep the Service running and fix problems.

Photos: we automatically strip location and other hidden data (EXIF) from photos you upload. We also keep a small fingerprint of each photo so copies of another member's photos can be refused. New public photos are checked by the site admin before other members can see them. Private-album photos are never reviewed.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking services, social-media tracking pixels or third-party cookies.

3.How we use it

  • To run the Service: show your profile and posts, deliver messages, keep you signed in, and send notifications you turned on.
  • To keep the community safe: confirm you're an adult, fight spam and ban evasion, enforce the Terms of Service, and review reports.
  • To email you codes and account notices. These include confirming your email, sign-in codes and important changes. We don't send marketing email.
  • To award trophies and show features like profile views and online status.
  • To process Gold Quill purchases and turn on the pass you bought.
  • To meet legal obligations. For example, we report child sexual abuse material to NCMEC and law enforcement.

Where privacy laws require a legal basis, we rely on running our agreement with you (the Terms), our legitimate interest in keeping the Service safe and working, legal obligations, and your consent for optional features like push notifications.

4.What other members can see

  • Always private: your real birthdate and age, email address, phone number, password, invite code and security signals.
  • Visible depending on your settings: your profile (to everyone or friends only), your friends list, your gifts, and your online status. Change these in Settings › Privacy.
  • Visible to people in the same place: your member name, profile picture and room messages, to others in that room. Profile comments and statuses are visible to whoever can see them under your settings.
  • Profile views: when you view someone's profile, its owner can see that you visited and when.
  • Character details you add, such as the character's age, city and gender, are shown on your profile. These are fiction, not facts about you.

Anything you post in a room can be seen, and possibly copied, by the people there. Share real-life details carefully.

5.Private messages and moderation

Private messages and private-album photos are visible only to the members involved. Staff don't browse them. Moderators see content only when it is reported, or when automated safety checks flag it. They can also look at content when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety. Administrative actions are recorded in an internal audit log.

6.Cookies and device storage

We use only what the site needs to work:

  • sid: your sign-in session cookie (lasts up to 30 days). Only a hash of it is stored on our side.
  • did: a random device cookie used for security, such as spotting ban evasion.
  • Browser storage: your theme and text size, a random device id (for the same security purpose), and the last message you saw in each room.

None of these are used for advertising or shared with anyone. Clearing your browser data removes them. You'll then need to sign in again.

7.Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that run the Service for us, under their own privacy and security terms:
    • Render (web hosting and temporary cache, USA)
    • Neon (database hosting, USA)
    • Resend (sending our emails, USA)
    • PayPal (payments, only if you buy a Gold Quill pass). PayPal handles your payment under its own privacy policy; we tell PayPal only what you're buying, the price and an internal account number to match the payment to you.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla), only if you turn on push notifications. Notifications never include message text.
  • Authorities, when required by law or needed to protect someone's safety. This includes reports of child sexual abuse material to NCMEC.
  • A successor, if the Service is ever transferred to someone else. This policy would continue to protect your information.

8.How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them or your account, or until your account has gone 2 years without a sign-in. Then it's deleted with all its data, after an email warning 30 days before.
  • Member rooms: deleted, with their messages, after 7 days without any messages.
  • Security signals: up to 90 days after they were last seen.
  • Profile-view records: 90 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days. Email codes expire within minutes.
  • When you delete your account: your profile, photos, messages (room and private), comments, statuses, friendships and other personal data are removed right away. Gifts you sent stay with their recipients, but no longer show your name.
  • What we keep after deletion: your member name, reserved so nobody can impersonate you; a minimal record of the deletion, of any moderation actions and of any purchases (order number, pass, amount and date, as accounting and tax rules require); and copies in short-term database backups until they cycle out.
  • Sanctioned accounts: an account under an active site-wide ban or mute can't be deleted until the sanction ends, so the record can't be wiped to dodge it.

9.Security

We protect your information with:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS), including to our database;
  • hashed passwords, session tokens and security signals;
  • optional two-factor sign-in;
  • rate limits;
  • staff access limited to what moderation needs.

No system is perfectly secure. Use a unique password and turn on two-factor sign-in. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll notify you as the law requires.

10.Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information: your account details are in Settings › Account, and your profile is in Edit profile. Your birthdate and phone number can't be changed once set. Contact us if one is wrong.
  • Control visibility: set your profile, friends list, gifts and online status in Settings › Privacy.
  • Delete things: remove posts, photos and gifts yourself, or delete your whole account in Settings.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications and in-site alerts on or off in Settings.
  • Legal rights: depending on where you live, including California and the EU/UK, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, to object to or restrict some uses of it, and to complain to a data-protection authority. To make a request, contact us. We'll verify it's really you and respond within the time the law requires. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

11.Adults only

The Service is only for people 18 and older, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its information, except what we must keep for safety or legal reasons. If you believe a minor is using the Service, please report it.

12.Where your data lives

The Service and its data are hosted in the United States. If you use it from another country, your information is transferred to and processed in the U.S., where privacy laws may differ from yours.

13.Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also tell members on the site before the change takes effect.
